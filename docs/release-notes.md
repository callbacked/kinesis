# kinesis 0.4.0

adds an air cursor (experimental). move your forearm to move the pointer, pinch your index to click or hold it to drag, and pinch your middle finger to right click. turn on developer mode at the bottom of band settings, then find it on the cursor page. hold option to move your arm without moving the pointer, and press escape to turn it off.

you can tune speed, flick boost, and steadiness on the cursor page. the defaults should feel right on either wrist without calibrating.

reconnecting after you reopen kinesis is quicker.
